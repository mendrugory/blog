This week I needed to find out where users were connecting from to one of our platforms at Palmtree Statistics. The HTTP request always provides the client's IP, which was enough, but I wanted to get something more.

[Torip](http://torip.readthedocs.org/) is an open source project based on the [Tornado Networking Library](http://www.tornadoweb.org/en/stable/networking.html). Torip gives you information such as the city, the country, or a Google Maps URL pointing to the client's location.

- [Github](https://github.com/mendrugory/torip)
- [ReadTheDocs](http://torip.readthedocs.org/en/latest/)
- [Pypi](https://pypi.python.org/pypi/torip)

If you use the [Tornado Web Framework](http://www.tornadoweb.org/en/stable/index.html) and Python 3, you can take advantage of Torip.

Enjoy!
